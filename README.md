# mCaffeine Marketing Strategy – Product & Business Case Study

## 1. Case Study Overview
A real customer experience that led to an analysis of
mCaffeine's promotional and marketing strategy.

## 2. Customer Observation
₹1,526 spent on 3 paid products + 7 additional products at ₹0.

## 3. Business Question
How can a brand use free products as part of its
customer acquisition, product sampling and cross-selling strategy?

## 4. Observed Strategy
- Product Sampling
- Cross-Selling
- Perceived Value
- Customer Retention
- Promotional Offers

## 5. Product Hypotheses
Freebies may encourage:
Trial → Product Discovery → Repeat Purchase

But they may also create:
Offer Dependence → Waiting for Discounts

## 6. Metrics to Evaluate
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Freebie → Paid Conversion
- Average Order Value
- Full-Price Conversion
- Customer Acquisition Cost
- Customer Lifetime Value

## 7. Key Product Insight
A promotion should not only be evaluated by
how much value it gives the customer.

It should be evaluated by the customer behaviour
it creates after the purchase.

## 8. Limitations
This case study is based on an individual customer
experience and publicly available information.

Internal costs, margins and customer-level data
are not available, so profitability is not concluded.

## 9. Conclusion
Marketing, product strategy, customer behaviour
and business metrics are closely connected.# mcaffeine-marketing-strategy-case-study
